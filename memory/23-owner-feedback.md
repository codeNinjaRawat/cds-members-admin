## Owner direction (chat, 10/17) – applies to every item above

### Rule: build for the big picture, not for one client
Every change from these recordings must be designed so another organization could think about it differently and
still use the product. Concretely:
- Nothing ISGH-specific is hard-wired. Zones, masjid names and addresses, membership plans, the Check-o-matic rule,
  the lifetime-by-donation threshold, the "name must match ID" policy, approval + payment rules, elected offices and
  term rules are all **configuration** (Settings / Membership rules / Masjids and zones / Elections), with ISGH's real
  values shipped as the demo preset ("Use ISGH's current levels" style) – never as the only option.
- Each rule has an on/off switch and its numbers are editable (e.g. qualifying total $40 and cut-off June 30;
  lifetime donation $25,000; term length 2 years; election month; one-office-per-person).
- Imports (Wild Apricot, Zeffy) are "import from a spreadsheet/export" with a field-mapping step, so a client coming
  from another system (Breeze, Planning Center, a plain Excel list) uses the same screen.
- Wording stays generic where it reaches the UI ("Islamic center / masjid" naming, zone labels, office titles are
  text the client sets). The SaaS app already carries per-organization settings – these items extend that, they do
  not bypass it.

### E1 NEW – Upload elected members, per year and per position
Requested by the Owner (chat). An upload/import in Elected officials (admin › Elected officials, module `officials`)
that loads **who held each position in each year**, so the full history of elections is in the system, not just the
current holders.
- Input: a spreadsheet (CSV / XLSX; a downloadable template) with one row per year × position × holder:
  year (or term start–end), position (office / Zonal Director / Associate Director / Area Representative /
  Secretary – or any title the client configured), zone / center / ZIP(s) where the seat applies, holder name,
  member ID (optional, matched to the members list), how they got the seat (elected / appointed / acting),
  oath date, end date, end reason (optional).
- Mapping step: columns matched to our fields, preview with row-level validation (unknown position, unknown
  center, overlapping terms for the same seat, one person in two elected offices in the same term), fix-or-skip.
- Result: a per-year view ("Term 2024–2026", "2022–2024" …) and a per-position history ("President: 2026 …,
  2024 …, 2022 …"); current holders stay what the existing screen shows; past terms are read-only history and feed
  the website's leadership page (optional "past leadership" section) and the Elections module (eligibility /
  term-limit counts such as `boardTerms` can be computed from the history instead of typed).
- Big-picture: positions and years come from the client's configuration (office list, term length, which years a
  seat is on the ballot); an organization with one-year terms or different titles uploads the same file.
- Also allow a manual "Add past term" form for one record and an export of the history in the same layout.
- Tests: black-box (upload the template, see the history; a bad row is reported and skipped) and white-box
  (overlap / one-office validations, year grouping).
- Status: **logged, not started** – waiting for "go" like the other items.
